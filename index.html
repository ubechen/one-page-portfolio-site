EN

About Jack Chen

I'm a Product / UX Designer with 8+ years of experience in B2B, B2B2C, complex systems, and hardware-software integrated products. My work spans AI PCs and new-form-factor laptops, service robots, drone ground control systems, enterprise websites, and a 0→1 health-tech product.

I'm most effective when the problem is still unclear. I use user research, market and competitor analysis, workshops, task flows, information architecture, wireframes, prototypes, GUI design, and field validation to turn fragmented information into product directions that teams can discuss, test, and build.

One of the most meaningful projects in my career was Angleasy, a wearable health-tech product I proposed at an internal hackathon that grew into a four-year, formal cross-functional project. I contributed to product definition, market and competitor research, regulatory exploration, and App/Web UX, and drove validation with 10+ partner organizations across 12 exhibitions. The product's two core inventions were granted patents in Taiwan, the EU, Japan, the US, and China.

I also apply generative AI to research synthesis, concept exploration, visual content, and rapid prototyping. I hold the Taiwan iPAS AI Application Planner certification and the III Generative AI Competency Certification, and I continue improving my AI knowledge and tool proficiency.

My current focus remains Product / UX Design, especially complex systems, B2B products, multi-device experiences, AI products, IoT, and industrial platforms. I am also open to Digital Product Manager or Product Owner opportunities where I can bring research, product discovery, system thinking, and cross-functional collaboration closer to product planning and decision-making.

中文

關於 陳泰運 (Jack Chen)

我是陳泰運 (Jack Chen)，一位擁有 8 年以上經驗的 Product／UX Designer，長期參與 B2B、B2B2C、複雜系統與軟硬體整合產品，專案涵蓋 AI PC 與創新型態筆電、服務型機器人、無人機地面控制站、企業網站，以及 0→1 健康科技產品。

我擅長在需求尚未明確時，透過使用者研究、市場與競品分析、工作坊、資訊架構、Task Flow、Wireframe、Prototype、GUI 與場域驗證，把零散資訊整理成可討論、可測試、可開發的產品方向。

對我職涯影響最深的專案之一，是由我提出概念的 Angleasy 穿戴式健康科技產品。它從公司內部黑客松發展為長達四年的正式跨職能專案，我參與產品定義、市場與競品研究、醫材法規探索、App／Web UX，並推動與 10+ 個合作單位、12 場展覽的驗證與推廣。產品的兩項核心發明分別取得台灣、歐盟、日本、美國與中國專利。

近年我持續將生成式 AI 應用於資料整理、概念探索、視覺內容與快速製作，並取得經濟部 iPAS「AI 應用規劃師初級」及資策會「生成式 AI 能力認證」，也會持續增進 AI 知識與工具熟練度。

目前仍以 Product／UX Designer 為主要方向，特別關注複雜系統、B2B 產品、跨裝置體驗、AI 產品、IoT 與工業平台；同時也開放 Digital Product Manager 或 Product Owner 的機會，希望把研究、產品探索、系統思考與跨部門協作能力，進一步帶到產品規劃與決策端。
